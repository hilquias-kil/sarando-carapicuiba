# Image consent: review what is published, start collecting authorização

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

The site publishes photographs of identifiable people, including children, and nobody knows whether any authorização de uso de imagem was ever collected. The redesign makes this worse before it makes it better: direction C runs photographs full-bleed, so every image gets larger and more prominent.

Two pieces of work, decided on the legal-pages ticket.

**1. Start collecting, from the next ação onward.** A written authorização de uso de imagem, one page, signed at the ação. For anyone under 18, signed by the responsável. This is a clipboard and a stack of paper, and it makes every future photograph safe to use without anyone having to think about it again.

**2. Review what is already published, narrowly.** Not a purge and not blurring. Group shots of an entrega read as documentary and stay. What comes down is the close-up where **one identifiable child is plainly the subject**, unless someone on the diretoria can name the guardian who agreed.

The photo library has redundancy, so the expected cost is a handful of images. Go through `static/actions/` and the four ação pages with a board member who was actually at each ação, since they are the only people who can answer the guardian question.

**This does not block the rebuild**, but it should be done before launch rather than after, because launch is the moment the photographs get bigger.

The privacy policy already carries the other half: anyone who appears, or whose child appears, can have a photo removed by writing one e-mail, without explaining why. That promise needs someone reading that inbox.
