# Inscrever a associação no CMDCA de Carapicuíba

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

The corporate-giving research turned up something worth more than the website. Donations routed through the municipal **Fundo dos Direitos da Criança e do Adolescente** are deducted **from imposto devido** rather than from lucro: 1% do IRPJ for lucro real companies and 6% for pessoa física. The donor gets back the entire amount, against roughly 34% in the best case under Lei 9.249/95, and unlike art. 13 it reaches individual donors too.

**The association is not registered.** Carapicuíba's CMDCA exists and the Sarando does not appear among the inscribed OSCs in Resolução CMDCA-Carapicuíba nº 01/2025. Without registration there is no chancela de projeto and no access to the fund. ECA art. 91 also says a non-governmental entity "somente poderá funcionar depois de registrada" with the council.

- [ ] Contact the CMDCA. Details found in council publications, **to be confirmed by phone before anything is published**: Rua Ângela Perioto Tolaine, 559, Centro; cmdca@carapicuiba.sp.gov.br; (11) 4146-4450 / 4164-5435.
- [ ] Assemble what registration requires: estatuto registrado, ata de eleição da diretoria, CNPJ, plano de trabalho, comprovação de instalações. Overlaps almost entirely with the estatuto-recovery ticket.
- [ ] Register the association, then inscribe a project for chancela.
- [ ] Confirm the retention percentage the local council applies (CONANDA Resolução 137/2010 set a minimum of 20%; local practice unconfirmed).

Cost is zero in fees, a few weeks in paperwork, renewed annually.

**This does not block the rebuild.** The site ships without it. What it blocks is the strongest argument the `/empresas` page could ever make, and until the association is registered that argument cannot appear on the site at all. Sequencing note: this and the estatuto ticket want the same documents, so do them together.
