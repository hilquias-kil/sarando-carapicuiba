# Recover the estatuto social

Type: task

## Question

The institutional-documents ticket came back with "we do not have it now" for the estatuto social. That is almost certainly a *retrieval* problem rather than an existence problem: a Brazilian association cannot hold CNPJ 05.392.117/0001-81 without a registered estatuto — it was filed at a cartório de registro civil de pessoas jurídicas to obtain it, and the contador who handles the association's obligations very likely has a copy.

This matters more than any other missing item, because the corporate donor path has lost both metrics and documents. The estatuto is the single artifact that most changes a CSR analyst's answer, and it costs nothing to publish once found.

- [ ] Ask the contador for a digital copy.
- [ ] Failing that, request a certidão/segunda via from the cartório where the association was registered.
- [ ] Confirm whether the association is willing to publish it publicly (most do; it is a public registry document).

**This ticket does not block the donor path.** If it cannot be recovered, the donor path proceeds without it — but it should proceed knowing the answer, not assuming one. The same question applies, more weakly, to the two termos de doação: those are documents the association *writes* rather than recovers, and a one-page termo is not hard to produce.

**Added by the voice guide:** the estatuto also records the **founding date**, which is still unconfirmed. Under the site's facts rule no year may be published without a source, so "fundada em ____" cannot appear until this document turns up or someone confirms the date another way. It is likely the most persuasive free fact the association owns and it currently appears nowhere on the site.
- [ ] Confirm the founding / registration year.
